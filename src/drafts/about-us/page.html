Our story

Who we are, what we believe, and the people who guide the Foundation.

Humble beginning

A family's commitment

Professor T. D. Dwivedi of Concordia University and his son, Dr. Shivendra Dwivedi, founded the Foundation to meet the educational and healthcare needs of underserved communities.

Our values · 4

  • Dignity

    Families gain respect and develop a sense of pride and success.

  • Passion

    Respect for human rights, with dedication and openness.

  • Independence

    Underprivileged people develop total independence.

  • Equality

    Everyone treated equally, regardless of gender, age, religion or status.
