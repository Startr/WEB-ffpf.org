Breaking The Poverty Cycle Of The Most Disadvantaged

We Transform Lives Through Access to Education and Healthcare

The Freedom from Poverty Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to breaking the cycle of poverty. Through medical, educational, and community-based initiatives, we empower families to create opportunities for sustainable growth and independence.

Every dollar you contribute directly impacts communities, improving quality of life and helping people achieve long-term resilience and self-reliance.

Our mission is powered by a global network of dedicated volunteers, including doctors, nurses, occupational therapists, public health professionals, accountants, students, and experts from diverse fields. These passionate individuals generously share their time and skills to help us realize our vision of “breaking the cycle of poverty for the most disadvantaged.”
